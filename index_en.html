⚠️

Important: Indoor Shoes Required (Badminton & Table Tennis)

Participants in badminton and table tennis must bring clean indoor athletic shoes. Outside shoes or dirty shoes are strictly prohibited inside the gymnasium — you will not be allowed to enter without indoor shoes.

日本語: バドミントンおよび卓球に参加される方は、必ず室内用のきれいな運動靴をご持参ください。

中文: 参加羽毛球以及乒乓球活动的人员请务必携带一双干净的室内运动鞋，否则无法进入球馆。

📅 Activity Details & How to Join

🏸 Badminton

Oi Elementary School — Fixed Saturday Evening Session

📍 Venue: Fujimino City Oi Elementary School Gymnasium

📅 Time: Every Saturday 18:00–20:00

💰 Fee: ¥100 per session

👥 Features: Beginners welcome · Many international members

Fixed practice every Saturday night! We enjoy basics and casual games in a friendly atmosphere. Please email us before your first visit.
⚠️ Please bring clean indoor athletic shoes. ※ Sessions may be cancelled without notice. First-time visitors must contact us in advance to avoid a wasted trip.

🎾 Tennis

Beginner Tennis (Flexible Booking)

📍 Venue: Fujimino City Sports Center Tennis Courts / Fujimino City Sports Park

📅 Time: Weekend evenings mainly (daytime possible when cooler)

💰 Fee: ¥400 per session

👥 Features: All beginners · Prior consultation required

In summer, we play on cool weekend evenings only. Tennis is held irregularly on weekends — email us to arrange a time and let's play together!
※ Sessions may be cancelled without notice. First-time visitors must contact us in advance to avoid a wasted trip.

🏓 Table Tennis

Beginner Table Tennis · Casual Meetup

📍 Venue: Fujimino 2nd Sports Park / Komagayashi Gym / Uenodai Gym / Fujimino General Sports Center

📅 Time: Weekends only (schedule by email)

💰 Fee: ¥100 per session

👥 Features: Irregular · Beginner-friendly

Table tennis is also weekends only, held irregularly (schedule by email). We use city gymnasium facilities. Feel free to email and let's find a time!
⚠️ Please bring clean indoor athletic shoes. ※ Sessions may be cancelled without notice. First-time visitors must contact us in advance to avoid a wasted trip.
